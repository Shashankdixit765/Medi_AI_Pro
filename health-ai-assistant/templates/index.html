Evidence-Based First Aid Protocols

Adult CPR (Cardiac Arrest)

Hands-Only CPR Protocol
  1. Check responsiveness and breathing. Call emergency dispatch immediately.
  2. Place heel of one hand in center of patient's chest; place other hand on top and interlock fingers.
  3. Push hard and fast: 100 to 120 compressions per minute at 2 inches (5 cm) depth.
  4. Allow chest to recoil fully between compressions. Continue until AED or ambulance arrives.

Stroke F.A.S.T. Assessment

Immediate Neurological Protocol
  • F - Face: Ask patient to smile. Look for one-sided facial drooping.
  • A - Arms: Ask them to raise both arms. Does one arm drift downward?
  • S - Speech: Ask them to repeat a simple sentence. Is speech slurred?
  • T - Time: Call emergency services immediately. Note exact symptom onset time!

Choking (Heimlich Maneuver)

Airway Obstruction Protocol
  1. Stand behind victim, wrap arms around their waist.
  2. Make a fist with one hand; place thumb side against abdomen just above navel.
  3. Grasp fist with other hand and press inward and upward with quick thrusts.
  4. Repeat until the foreign object is expelled or patient becomes unconscious (begin CPR).

Heart Attack Protocol

Suspected Myocardial Infarction
  • Have patient immediately stop all physical exertion and sit in a comfortable resting position.
  • Dial emergency medical dispatch without delay.
  • Administer 300mg chewed Aspirin unless history of active bleeding or allergy.
  • Loosen tight collars or clothing around chest and neck. Keep patient calm.

Severe Allergic Anaphylaxis

Acute Airway Hypersensitivity
  • Look for swollen lips/tongue, wheezing, hives, or sudden dizziness.
  • Immediately inject Epinephrine auto-injector (EpiPen) into outer mid-thigh.
  • Lay patient flat with legs elevated (unless breathing is difficult, then sit up).
  • Call emergency hotline immediately; second dose may be needed after 5-15 mins.

Severe Hemorrhage / Bleeding

External Wound Hemostasis
  • Apply firm, continuous direct pressure over the wound using sterile gauze or clean cloth.
  • Maintain constant pressure for at least 10 minutes without lifting gauze to check.
  • Apply pressure bandage. If arterial spurting limb bleeding persists, apply commercial tourniquet 2-3 inches above wound.
  • Keep patient warm to prevent hypothermia and shock.
